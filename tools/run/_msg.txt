art(batch 1) + wiring: real doors, chests, altars, stone floors; sound hears itself correctly

Art (by art-batch, task-3): 18 assets under assets/props, assets/decor and assets/tiles, plus a
contact sheet and report in tools/design/concepts/. Every asset is inside +-15% of its
authorised pixel height, magenta residue 0, empty frames 0.

Engine wiring (this commit)
- room_builder loads assets/tiles/floor_<theme>.png and wall_stone.png when present, and falls
  back to the procedural textures when they are not. The fallback stays permanently: it keeps
  the project runnable while art is being generated, and a missing file degrades the look
  rather than breaking the run.
- prop_util.sprite() and contact_shadow(): props draw as sprites when art exists and as the
  original boxes when it does not. Door, chest and altar each got both paths, and the lit altar
  and open chest swap in a second sprite rather than rotating a drawing -- a lid rotating on a
  painted chest tears, because the art is a drawing of a chest and not a rig.
- prop_util.sprite() takes an optional width_m. Height-locked scaling is wrong for a door: the
  drawing is 1.60 x 2.28 m while the opening is 1.60 x 2.60 m, so scaling by height would push
  it 11 cm into the wall on each side and the doorway would read as walled up. Width-locked, the
  door is 2.29 m in a 2.6 m opening, which is just a door with a frame above it.

Sound correctness -- both found by the hearing assertions, not by listening
- The opposition was alerting itself. A cultist's idle chant went through the same noise_made
  signal as a player action, so a single chant cascaded through the building and the player's
  own noise stopped being the thing that mattered. Sound.play() now takes a `source`, and only
  the player has ears.
- tests/shot.gd's hearing assertions were measuring the wrong thing twice. The check that a
  quiet noise does not wake a cultist was passing against a subject left mid-investigation by
  the door and chest noises earlier in the same run -- a false green, which is worse than a
  failure. And a noise dropped 1.2 m away was heard and instantly forgotten, because the
  cultist was already inside INVESTIGATE_ARRIVE and treated it as reached. Both comments now
  record the geometry, and the test asserts the boundary from both sides: heard at 2.0 m with a
  2.55 m earshot, not heard at 4.5 m.

Also: tools/run/kill-godot.ps1. The console wrapper exits while the GUI child keeps running, so
interrupted runs leave processes behind; six had accumulated, and one of them was probably why a
run came back exit 1 with no output at all.
