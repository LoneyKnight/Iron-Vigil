feat: lantern-and-candle lighting, noise meter, camera re-solved after the art landed

Camera (tools/art/sweep.py -> tools/design/concepts/camera_sweep.png)
Re-swept pitch against the real art instead of the grey boxes the framing was solved on. Pitch
28 gives the strongest depth, but a 7 m room does not fill the frame at that angle and the view
reads as a corridor looking through walls; 34 flattens the room; 46 is a floor plan again. 36 is
kept, and the measured framing is unchanged: 85 px/m, knight 123.8 px, wall face 316.3 px.

Lighting: a warm lantern against a cool ambient
The colour split is the whole design and it was missing. The stone reads blue-grey where nothing
is lighting it and the pool around the player is tallow-yellow, so the lantern REVEALS the room
instead of merely brightening it. With a neutral ambient both values are the same grey and the
lantern becomes a brightness control. Fog trimmed and re-tinted to carry depth without washing
out the architecture.

Noise meter (HUD): the resource the player spends, made visible
Blocks, a word, and the earshot it buys. Without it, "running is loud" is an instruction the
player has to be told; with it, it is a dial they watch while deciding. It decays, because the
state that matters is how loud they are right now.

A ceiling was tried and removed, and the reason is recorded in the code
A flat slab overhead is the obvious way to make a room feel enclosed, and it is wrong in this
view: the camera looks down at the player, the player sits at the centre of the frame, and the
centre of the frame is exactly where a ceiling plane crosses it. Measured result: the top half of
the room hidden and the figure sliced in two. What sells interior is vertical architecture, which
is also what the reference material does.

The wall UV scale was briefly halved to quieten the masonry, and reverted. It also doubled the
apparent block size and the coursing stopped matching the floor. If the walls read as busy, the
fix belongs in the texture, not in the scale.

Testing, and a lesson about proxies
Two hearing assertions now measure the behaviour instead of a proxy for it:
- "the cultist walks toward the noise" samples its VELOCITY direction while it investigates
  (76 of 76 sampled frames) rather than its net displacement. Three attempts at a displacement
  threshold all failed intermittently for reasons unrelated to hearing: a wandering cultist swaps
  to a random target every few seconds, and one that arrives inside INVESTIGATE_ARRIVE stops on
  purpose, so displacement can honestly be zero after walking straight there.
- The hearing test parks the player 5 km away, because a 6 m sight cone was reaching the noise
  position on some seeds and the test was measuring chase behaviour instead of hearing.

Also: tools/art/sweep.py looked for "<name>.png" while the test writes "<name>_start.png", so
every sweep reported "no screenshot produced" and the sweep looked broken when it was not. And
tools/run/kill-godot.ps1 exists because the console wrapper exits while the GUI child keeps
running: six processes had accumulated, and one of them was probably why a run returned exit 1
with no output at all.
